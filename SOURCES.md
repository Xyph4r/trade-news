# SOURCES.md: Where the briefing gets its news

Tiers say how far a source can be trusted. Prefer tier 1 for facts and numbers,
use tier 2 for reporting and context, use tier 3 for prices and calendars.

## Tier 1: Primary and official sources

| Topic | Sources |
|---|---|
| Central banks | federalreserve.gov, ecb.europa.eu, boj.or.jp, bankofengland.co.uk, pbc.gov.cn, snb.ch, rbi.org.in, bundesbank.de |
| Statistics | bls.gov, bea.gov, census.gov, eurostat (ec.europa.eu/eurostat), destatis.de, ons.gov.uk, stats.gov.cn, e-stat.go.jp |
| German sentiment surveys | ifo.de, zew.de |
| International bodies | imf.org, worldbank.org, oecd.org, bis.org, wto.org |
| Rates and FX data | fred.stlouisfed.org, ECB reference rates (ecb.europa.eu), treasury.gov |
| Commodities | eia.gov (oil and gas), iea.org, lbma.org.uk (gold and silver), opec.org |
| Companies | Company investor-relations pages, sec.gov (EDGAR), regulatory news (ad-hoc notices under EU market abuse rules) |
| Funds and ETFs | Issuer pages: ishares.com / blackrock.com, fidelity.lu / fidelity.de, am.jpmorgan.com, schroders.com, swisscanto.com; index providers msci.com, stoxx.com |

## Tier 3: Prices, calendars, overviews

- Prices and quotes: finanzen.net, onvista.de, boerse-frankfurt.de, investing.com,
  finance.yahoo.com, stooq.com, marketwatch.com
- Economic calendar: investing.com/economic-calendar, tradingeconomics.com/calendar,
  forexfactory.com/calendar
- Earnings calendars: nasdaq.com/market-activity/earnings, finanzen.net/termine
- Funds and ETFs: justetf.com, morningstar.de, fondsweb.com

Check figures from tier 3 against a second source when they are central to a story.

## Blocklist

Do not use these as a source for facts:

- Social media posts (X/Twitter, Reddit, Telegram, TikTok, StockTwits) unless the
  post comes from the official account of a central bank, government or company,
  and then link the official account.
- Paid stock tips, "Börsenbriefe", penny-stock promotion and sponsored articles
  (look for "Anzeige", "Sponsored", "Promoted", "Advertorial").
- Content farms and AI-written aggregator sites without named authors.
- Price predictions from unknown sites ("XYZ stock forecast 2030").
