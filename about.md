This site publishes a short briefing on global markets every weekday morning
(Berlin time) and a longer weekly recap every Monday.

The briefings are written automatically by Claude, an AI model, from public news
sources and official data, and every story links its source. Figures can be
delayed or incomplete; check the linked source before relying on a number.

**Nothing on this site is investment advice.**
