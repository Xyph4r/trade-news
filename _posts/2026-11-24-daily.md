## Top stories

1. **Huang is back, and so is the AI trade.** Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, citing input from senior US officials that his return poses no national security risk; President Trump had pushed for it. Nvidia closed about 31% higher, and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq had their best day since April 2025. Some analysts welcomed the end of the uncertainty; others say the board's U-turn, a week after ruling out his return, raises questions about its judgement. The California investigation is still open. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-americas-market-news-wrap-nvidia-climbs-30-as-jensen-huang-returns/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/analysts-cheer-huang-s-nvidia-return-but-question-the-board-s-sudden-u-turn/))

2. **Asia rebounds, China lags.** The Kospi jumped about 7% back above 5,000, led by SK Hynix (+8.6%) and Samsung Electronics (+8.2%), and the Nikkei rose about 4% after Japan's holiday. Chinese stocks edged up from a 16-month low despite record state buying of 90 billion yuan of ETFs on Friday, and the Hang Seng slipped to its weakest since April 2025. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-huang-s-return-powers-asia-s-ai-rebound/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/icymi-china-s-90-billion-yuan-rescue-record-state-etf-buying-struggles-to-halt-ai-sell-off/))

3. **Germany's economy surprises on the upside.** The flash composite [PMI](/learn/pmi/) rose to 55.0 from 53.4, its highest since March 2022 and well above the 53.2 forecast. Manufacturing reached a 56-month high of 55.0 and services rose to 54.0. Service firms are still cutting jobs, and cost inflation eased to a three-month low. ([Reuters via 104.9 The Wolf](https://www.1049thewolf.com/2026/11/23/german-business-activity-growth-accelerates-in-november-pmi-shows/))

4. **Oil ignores Hormuz headlines.** Brent posted its lowest close since 31 August and WTI dipped below $80. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (not independently confirmed), but the market focused on supply: Deutsche Bank estimates an oversupply of 1.7 million barrels a day in October, and Libya's Sharara pipeline has reopened. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/commodities/iran-says-three-tankers-hit-mines-yet-oil-sits-near-a-three-month-low-as-supply-wins/))

5. **The Fed debate continues.** New York Fed President John Williams said he would not rule out hikes at consecutive meetings, while calling 2027 a year of lower inflation. BlackRock argues markets price too many hikes and expects December plus one more in the first half of 2027 (forecast). The decision is on 9 December. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-huang-s-return-powers-asia-s-ai-rebound/))
