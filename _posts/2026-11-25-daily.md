## Top stories

1. **Tariffs are back.** The Wall Street Journal reported that the Trump administration is preparing new tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies after an investigation into industrial subsidies, with findings possibly this week (reported, not yet announced). The plan would cover most imports and could rise further if partners keep their industrial policies. investingLive notes this is inflationary while the Fed is already hiking; markets price about 26 bp for December. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/stock-markets-fall-as-trump-pivots-from-corporate-meddling-to-fresh-tariffs/))

2. **The AI rebound fades.** Nvidia fell 5.9% to $155.38 after Monday's 31% jump, and AI infrastructure names fell harder: Astera Labs -13.9%, Credo -13.1%, CoreWeave -11.9% and Micron -11.0%. The Dow (+0.9%) and Russell 2000 (+0.6%) rose as money moved into other sectors. President Trump said he had thought about, but not proposed, a US government stake in Nvidia. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-americas-fx-news-wrap-24-nov-it-s-a-wrap-chip-stocks-slide-anthropic-breaks-ipo-price-usd-lower/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/trump-floats-a-us-government-stake-in-nvidia-calling-the-chipmaker-vital-to-ai-leadership/))

3. **Asia sells chips again.** The Kospi lost almost 6%, giving back most of Monday's gain, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix each down more than 9% on foreign selling. The Nikkei fell nearly 3%, while the broader TOPIX was down less than 0.5%. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-ai-selloff-returns/))

4. **Inflation runs hot outside the US.** Australia's trimmed mean CPI rose to 3.8% in October (forecast 3.6%), with headline [inflation](/learn/inflation/) at 4.9%. Japan's services producer prices rose 4.2% (forecast 3.6%), supporting the case for a Bank of Japan hike in December. Bond yields still fell as investors sought safety. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-ai-selloff-returns/))

5. **German sentiment edges up.** The [ifo](/learn/ifo-and-zew/) business climate rose to 90.6 from a revised 90.1 (forecast 90.5). Services and trade improved, manufacturing slipped after six gains. It follows Monday's strong [PMI](/learn/pmi/). ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/european-stocks-fall-as-trump-turns-back-to-tariffs-italy-leads-the-declines/))
