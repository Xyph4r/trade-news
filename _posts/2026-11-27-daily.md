## Top stories

1. **Europe rallies on milder tariff plans.** The Euro Stoxx 50 and the DAX rose about 2% on Thursday, catching up with Wednesday's late US comeback. investingLive reports that the proposed US levies on 14 trading partners will not stack on top of the July tariffs and are unlikely to take effect before February or March, which eased fears. S&P 500 futures closed Thursday's session up 0.54%. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/european-stocks-surge-s-p-500-futures-finish-higher-in-thanksgiving-trading/))

2. **Asia's chip stocks lead a broad rebound.** The Kosdaq jumped about 6.7% and the Kospi about 4%, with SK Hynix up about 4.6% and Samsung Electronics about 4.2%. Nasdaq 100 futures were up about 2.4% during the Asian session. China's cabinet pledged new capital-market measures and a bigger role for the state-backed "national team" in stabilising stocks, but gave no figures. October industrial profits rose 20.1% year on year, led by electronics. ([investingLive, Asia wrap, Spanish edition](https://investinglive.com/es/noticias/investinglive-noticias-del-mercado-de-asia-pacifico-kosdaq-salta-un-67-a-medida-que-el-apetito-por-el-riesgo-regresa-el-oro-se-recupera/), [investingLive, China profits](https://investinglive.com/news/china-s-industrial-profits-jump-20-1-in-october-as-electronics-do-the-heavy-lifting/))

3. **Hernández de Cos picked to lead the ECB.** EU leaders chose Spain's Pablo Hernández de Cos, now head of the BIS, to succeed Christine Lagarde in February, according to The Wall Street Journal as reported by investingLive. The European Parliament and the ECB's Governing Council still have to give their views, with final approval expected in December. He beat the Netherlands' Klaas Knot. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/ecb-picks-spain-s-hernandez-de-cos-as-president-with-3-tests-waiting-from-day-one/))

4. **ECB accounts lean towards a hike.** The accounts of the 28 and 29 October meeting, when the deposit rate stayed at 2.50%, show that a large number of members said incoming data confirmed the case for further tightening and that they could have supported a hike in October. The next decision is in December. ([ECB accounts via ActionForex](https://www.actionforex.com/central-banks/european-central-bank/662223-ecb-monetary-policy-accounts-36/), [ECB decision 29 Oct](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.mp261029~9a493adf17.en.html))

5. **Tokyo inflation keeps a December BoJ hike in play.** Tokyo core CPI rose to 2.9% in November, a 16-month high, from 2.6%, just below the 3.0% forecast. The trend measure that excludes fresh food and energy eased to 2.5%. Markets expect a hike to 1.5% at the 17 and 18 December meeting (expected). USD/JPY held near 159. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/tokyo-core-inflation-hits-16-month-high-of-2-9-firming-the-case-for-a-december-boj-hike/))
