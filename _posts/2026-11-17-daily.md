## Top stories

1. **Third straight loss for the S&P 500.** The index fell 1.9% to 7,416.59, the Nasdaq Composite 2.5% and the Dow 1.4% to 51,608.17. Nvidia lost 4.1% and, according to AP, has shed $735 billion in market value since the scandal around former CEO Jensen Huang began. AMD fell 5.6%, Broadcom 3.9% and CoreWeave 6.1%. ([AP via WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/stocks-decline-asia/Y6KSUFUT6E4JJJKTTJKBZ6637U/))

2. **US closes the "remote access" loophole for AI chips.** The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim rule that requires a licence to ship advanced AI chips and servers to data centres anywhere that could give Chinese firms computing access. It also adds licence requirements for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil and formally rescinds the earlier AI Diffusion Rule. Reports differ on when it takes effect (immediately or 19 November). ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/16/nation/trump-administration-moves-close-ai-chip-loophole-before-china-trip/), [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/de/insights/2026/11/bis-interim-final-rule-targets-remote-access-to-advanced-semiconductors-rescinds-ai-diffusion-rule))

3. **Nvidia reports tonight.** It is the first set of results since Huang's resignation, with Colette Kress leading the call. CNBC reported on Monday that the board is not considering bringing Huang back as CEO. Investors are used to Nvidia beating forecasts and guiding above expectations. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-stock-price-jensen-huang.html), [AP via WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/stocks-decline-asia/Y6KSUFUT6E4JJJKTTJKBZ6637U/))

4. **Oil and yields ease.** Brent fell 3.5% to $92.44, which took some pressure off inflation worries. AP put the 10-year Treasury yield at 5.13% late Monday; FRED's daily series shows 5.12%. ([AP via WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/stocks-decline-asia/Y6KSUFUT6E4JJJKTTJKBZ6637U/))
