## Top stories

1. **Nvidia's leadership crisis keeps chip stocks on edge.** Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO and board member on Thursday, and CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. On Friday Nvidia fell another 2%, Broadcom 4.8% and AMD 8.8%, after both rivals had gained earlier in the week. The S&P 500 still ended the week 2.5% higher, its first weekly gain since early October. Two large shareholders, Altimeter and Atreides, wrote to the board on Friday asking it not to rush the search for a new CEO. ([AP via WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/asian-shares-are/HYJESTPHW47S7NSEMT63ALTKMY/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html))

2. **US producer prices up 6.5% on the year.** Final-demand producer prices rose 0.3% in October, a little below forecasts, but the annual rate of 6.5% is the highest since November 2022. With consumer inflation well above 3% as well, markets expect the Fed to raise rates at its December meeting. The 10-year Treasury yield closed at 5.15% on Friday. ([AP via WSB-TV](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/business/wholesale-inflation/S5LSKC44LU3ILE73B2SA2DUCRM/), [AP via ADN](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/11/13/stocks-sway-as-wall-street-heads-for-winning-week-after-several-turbulent-days/))

3. **Japan's economy shrank slightly in the third quarter.** Preliminary data released overnight showed real GDP down at an annualised 0.1% in July to September, against expectations of small growth. Private consumption fell and net exports weighed, as high oil prices from the Iran war squeeze households. ([AP via NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2026/11/16/japans-economy-contracts-slightly-in-the-latest-quarter-as-oil-prices-remain-high), [Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/japanese-gdp-growth-annualized-prel-q3--01-vs-exp-03-prev-14))

4. **China's October data disappoint.** Industrial output grew 4.8% on the year (forecast 5.3%) and retail sales just 0.4% (forecast 0.8%), the weakest consumer reading in months. ([The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/348591/Chinas-factory-output-retail-sales-growth-slow-in-October))
