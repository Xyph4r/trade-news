## Top stories

1. **Relief bounce in US futures.** S&P 500 futures were up about 2.2% near 7,090 and Nasdaq 100 futures close to 3% through the Asian session, after the S&P 500 lost 8.6% last week, its worst week since April 2025, and closed in a [correction](/learn/correction-and-bear-market/). investingLive described the tone as "relief, not recovery", with the Fed's 9 December decision still ahead. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-us-futures-hold-2-bounce))

2. **Seoul and Beijing step in.** Korea's Kospi fell almost 6% at its low, which triggered a "sidecar" pause on programme selling, before recovering to about -3%. Over the weekend Korean officials began preparing a market stabilisation fund of about 10.8 trillion won (about $8 billion), unused since 2008, eased buyback limits and announced a crackdown on illegal short selling. China's three exchanges cut the margin requirement for buying stocks on credit to 80% from 100%, effective 30 November. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-us-futures-hold-2-bounce))

3. **Nvidia: politics and probes pull in different directions.** President Trump wrote that he hopes Nvidia's board rehires Jensen Huang, and Treasury Secretary Bessent said a return depends on what investigators find. California's attorney general said his probe now covers possible criminal violations of state law; it is preliminary. A House committee also published data showing the US approved about 880,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips for Chinese firms from December 2025 to May 2026; Nvidia says it has stopped shipping H200s to China. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/california-widens-nvidia-probe-to-criminal-law-a-bigger-deal-than-huang-return-talk/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/us-approved-nearly-900-000-nvidia-and-amd-ai-chips-for-china-raises-more-questions-than-answers/))

4. **The Fed debate heats up.** Bessent warned the Fed may be making a mistake if it overtightens, and Bill Ackman called for a 3% inflation target. On the other side, Governor Waller wants a half-point hike or a signal of a January follow-up, and Goldman Sachs expects hikes in December and March. Futures still price a December hike as close to certain. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-us-futures-hold-2-bounce))

5. **Gold rebounds after its worst week in eight months.** Gold futures rose about 2.9% to $3,615.60 this morning after a 9.5% weekly drop, as the forced selling that drove last week's slide eased. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/commodities/gold-sinks-9-in-a-week-as-margin-calls-and-fed-hike-bets-override-its-haven-role/))
