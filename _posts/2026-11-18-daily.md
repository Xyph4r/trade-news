## Top stories

1. **Nvidia beats and raises in its first report without Huang.** Third-quarter revenue was $115.8 billion, up 103% on the year, with data-centre revenue of $107.6 billion. For the fourth quarter, which has an extra week, Nvidia expects about $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, and assumes no data-centre compute revenue from China. The shares rose more than 3% in after-hours trading. Earlier on Tuesday Jensen Huang wrote on X that he would return as CEO if the board decided that was in the company's interest; Nvidia is setting up a board committee for national security and trade compliance. ([Nvidia](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2027.html), [AP via KBTX](https://www.kbtx.com/2026/11/17/another-wipeout-hits-wall-street-nvidia-other-jittery-ai-stocks-swing-lower/))

2. **Fourth loss in a row on Wall Street.** The S&P 500 fell 1% to 7,340.53, the Dow 848.75 points (1.6%) to 50,759.42 and the Nasdaq Composite 0.7%. Home Depot fell 2.4% despite slightly better profit, Disney 1.5% and JPMorgan 2.4%. ([AP via KBTX](https://www.kbtx.com/2026/11/17/another-wipeout-hits-wall-street-nvidia-other-jittery-ai-stocks-swing-lower/))

The euro-area reading climbed to 26.5. European stocks still fell about 2% with the global tech sell-off. ([Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/german-zew-economic-sentiment-index-nov-356-vs-exp-29-prev-281), [Newsquawk, euro area](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/european-zew-economic-sentiment-index-nov-265-vs-exp-191-prev-185))

4. **US consumers keep spending.** Retail sales rose 0.2% in October, in line with forecasts, and AP reported that an underlying measure of spending grew more than economists expected. The 10-year yield briefly rose toward 5.15% before falling back to 5.10% as investors moved into bonds.
