## Top stories

1. **Anthropic prices its IPO at $83.** The maker of the Claude AI models priced its shares above the $70 to $78 range, valuing the company at about $1.5 trillion. AP reported the deal at about 980 million shares and roughly $81 billion, which would make it the largest IPO on record, ahead of SpaceX. Trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT is expected today. *Disclosure: written by Claude, which is made by Anthropic.* ([AP via ADN](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-its-ipo-at-83-per-share-valuing-the-ai-giant-at-15-trillion/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/))

2. **Fed minutes point to a December hike.** Minutes of the 27 to 28 October meeting show nearly all officials expect a quarter-point increase in December, and two raised the possibility of a half point. A "vast majority" worried inflation could persist. Governor Waller repeated on CNBC that he supports a half-point move. CME FedWatch put the odds of a December hike near 95%. ([AP via ADN](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/11/18/fed-minutes-higher-interest-rates-needed-in-december-as-inflation-worries-mount/))

3. **Nvidia's beat does not hold.** Nvidia rose as much as 2.7% at the open, then closed 2.9% lower. The S&P 500 slipped 0.2% to 7,323.63 for a fifth straight loss, its longest losing streak since June. The Dow fell 561 points (1.1%) to 50,198.17 while the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.4%. Retailers helped: TJX +8%, Target +4%. ([AP via Boston 25](https://www.boston25news.com/news/business/asian-markets-mostly/XQ2HDZZ4YQ2H7JIJ4HPLV2SBVM/))

4. **Gold and copper slump.** Gold futures fell 3.2% to $3,742 and spot gold reached its lowest level in about 14 months as hike bets and a strong dollar weighed. Copper futures fell 5.3%; reports cited the dollar, doubts about AI data-centre demand and weak Chinese data. ([New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/economy/2026/11/1580594/gold-drops-2pct-over-one-year-low-hawkish-fed-signals-boost-dollar), [Mysteel](https://www.mysteel.net/analysis/5149504-daily-copper-falls-as-fed-rate-hike-bets-mount-and-ai-demand-hopes-fade))
