## Top stories

1. **Hot inflation, strong growth, and a big intraday comeback.** October core PCE rose to 3.3% year on year (forecast 3.2%) and headline [inflation](/learn/inflation/) to 3.8%. Q3 [GDP](/learn/gdp/) growth was revised up to 4.3% annualised from 3.4%, and jobless claims fell to 189,000. The Dow was down 1,105 points at its low but closed up 0.33%; the S&P 500 recovered from -3.2% to -0.25%. Treasury yields still fell, the 10-year by 5.6 [basis points](/learn/basis-point/) to 4.845%, and a Fed hike on 9 December stays in play. ([investingLive, US close](https://investinglive.com/stocks/u-s-stocks-close-mixed-as-dow-reverses-a-1-105-point-drop/), [investingLive, US data](https://investinglive.com/stocks/european-stocks-close-lower-as-the-nasdaq-100-sheds-nearly-4/))

2. **Asia extends its rebound as two central banks lean hawkish.** The Bank of Korea raised its base rate by a quarter point to 3.25%, its third hike since July, with two dissents; 15 of 21 board projections sit above 3.25%. Bank of Japan board member Naoki Tamura said rates should reach neutral, which he puts near 2%, "soon"; Japan's 2-year yield rose about 4 bp. The Nikkei and Kospi gained about 1.5% or more, led by chipmakers. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-asia-stocks-extend-rebound/))

3. **The AI stress moves into credit.** S&P Global Ratings put Nvidia's AA rating on CreditWatch negative, citing board independence, key-person risk and export controls, while Moody's kept Aa1. Nvidia fell 3.9% on Wednesday; Lam Research (-5.7%) and Micron (-4.5%) also fell. CoreWeave's banks are preparing a $4.5 billion loan backed by a Meta contract (reported). ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/investinglive-asia-pacific-market-news-asia-stocks-extend-rebound/))

4. **Fed Beige Book: flat consumers, firmer wages.** Eight of twelve districts reported slight to moderate growth and four no change. Consumer spending was little changed, wages rose moderately, an uptick from the last report, and firms cited tariff-related costs among sustained price pressures. ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202611-summary.htm))

5. **Europe takes the tariff hit.** The Euro Stoxx 50 fell 1.8%, Spain's Ibex 2.0% and Italy's FTSE MIB 1.8%, as fresh US tariff proposals added to the AI sell-off. Bund and gilt yields fell almost 6 bp, but Italy's spread over Germany widened by about 5 bp. ([investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/european-stocks-close-lower-as-the-nasdaq-100-sheds-nearly-4/))
